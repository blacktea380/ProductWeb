Problem: Automation Lacking "Soul"

  • Single-dimension decisions: Relies only on RPM, ignoring the rider's real-time load and torque.
  • Closed preset logic: Shifting rules set by manufacturers; cannot be deeply optimized for physiological traits.
  • Communication latency bottleneck: Traditional protocols suffer stability and responsiveness issues in high-interference environments.

Solution: Dual-Input Sensing Engine

Force Sensing

Integrates high-precision strain gauges to detect pedal power and torque output—sense how hard you pedal.

Cadence (RPM)

Accurately records revolutions per minute to maintain optimal physiological performance zones.

Programmable Algorithms

Combining dual data inputs, users can customize shift thresholds for true contextual awareness.

Core Technology: ExpressLRS

Unrivaled Communication Bandwidth

Adopts an FPV-grade open-source wireless protocol with a **1000Hz refresh rate** and **ultra-low latency**.

  • Interference-resistant frequency hopping: Stable even with hundreds of bikes nearby.
  • Fast response: Latency only ~1/10 of traditional systems.
  • Open-source freedom: Firmware upgrades are fast and not locked by major manufacturers.

Product Features: Full Control

User-programmable logic

Custom rules: e.g., shift down automatically when force > Y and RPM < 80.

Multiple terrain profiles

Create dedicated shift-logic profiles for climbs, flats, and sprints.

Open ecosystem compatibility

Compatible with mainstream cassettes and cranks—no full-bike replacement required.

Software Enabled: Force-Shift App

Data-as-control

Through an intuitive graphical interface, riders can drag curves to tune shift sensitivity and timing. Shifting is no longer blind—it's data-driven energy management.
